Budget-Conscious Healthy Strategies

1. Buy Canned & Frozen Staples

Frozen vegetables retain the same nutrients as fresh produce at half the cost. Canned tuna, salmon, and chickpeas are affordable, shelf-stable protein options.

2. Batch Cooking & Grains

Cook large batches of brown rice, oats, or quinoa over the weekend. Buying grains in bulk lowers per-serving costs to under $0.40.

3. Rotate Plant Proteins

Replace meat with tofu, lentils, or black beans 2 to 3 days per week to cut your total food bill by up to 35% while keeping fiber high.
